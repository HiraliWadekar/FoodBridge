FOOD GUIDE

What Can Be Donated?

Basic guidance shown as a frontend awareness feature.

Generally Suitable

  • Freshly prepared meals
  • Sealed packaged food
  • Fresh fruits and vegetables
  • Unopened dry food items
Check condition first

Check Carefully

  • Cooked rice and grains
  • Dairy-based food
  • Prepared salads
  • Foods kept for several hours
Check time & storage

Do Not Donate

  • Spoiled or bad-smelling food
  • Mouldy food
  • Contaminated food
  • Food with damaged packaging
Safety comes first
